Frequently asked questions

Is Viasat Internet available where I live?

Viasat offers satellite internet service to the majority of the U.S., including in rural and remote locations where cable and fiber aren't available. Because it uses satellites in orbit instead of ground-based cables, it can reach areas that other terrestrial providers can't. You can check availability at your specific address here.

How is Viasat Internet different from other satellite internet providers?

Viasat's satellite network is backed by 40+ years of satellite communications experience and powered by some of the world's most advanced satellites.

Customers benefit from:

  • Live customer support available if you need help with your service or have any questions
  • Free standard installation from certified technicians who help to ensure an optimal connection
  • Flexible plan options, including no-contract plans, that meet the needs of your household

Explore satellite internet plans for your home here and satellite internet plans for your small business here.

Who is Viasat Internet best for?

Viasat Internet is a good option for households in areas where cable and fiber don't reach, including rural and remote areas. It works well for essential, everyday online activities like shopping, banking, education, and staying in touch with family, as well as streaming, video calls, remote work, browsing, and connecting multiple devices.

Viasat is a great option as a primary or backup connection for small businesses throughout the U.S. When cable and fiber are unavailable, Viasat provides primary connectivity to customers in many different industries. When businesses use other connection types as their primary connection, Viasat can be a great backup solution to help them stay online when their primary connection is disconnected by outages, disasters, or local infrastructure issues.
